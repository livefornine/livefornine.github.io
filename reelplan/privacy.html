Reelplan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Reelplan is made by Sofiia Lapchyk, an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your device

Reelplan stores your posts, ideas, scripts, captions and settings only on your phone. We do not have servers that receive this data, and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase history and basic device information (such as the operating system version and country). It does not receive your name or the content you create in the app. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Advertising and tracking

The app has no ads and does not track you across apps or websites.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Your rights

Because your content stays on your device, you control it fully. For questions about data processed by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, you can contact us and we will help.

Changes

We will update this page if the app starts handling data differently.

Він отримує анонімний ідентифікатор користувача, історію покупок і базові дані про пристрій (версію системи, країну). Твоє ім'я і вміст, який ти створюєш у застосунку, він не отримує.
